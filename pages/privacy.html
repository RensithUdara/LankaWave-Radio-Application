Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

LankaWave stores only two things locally on your device:

  • Your last selected station
  • Your volume level

This data never leaves your browser. We do not use cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts.

Audio streams

When you play a station, your browser connects directly to that station's stream server. LankaWave does not proxy or record any audio.

Third-party services

We load fonts from Google Fonts. Google's privacy policy applies to that service.

Contact
